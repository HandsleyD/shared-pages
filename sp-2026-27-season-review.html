Overachievers So Far

Hearts — 3rd, 13 pts. New manager Wouter Vrancken has them firing at home: a perfect record through three games (9 pts). They were written off before kickoff but are firmly in the "three-horse race" conversation alongside Celtic and Rangers. If this runs to form, expect another Hearts shock.
Dundee — 4th, 10 pts. Unseeded and unheralded at the start of the season, but an early-season win streak got them into a comfortable mid-table groove with more than a point per game. Tough to maintain against tougher runs, so far so good.
St Mirren — 5th, 10 pts. Also over-performing relative to expectations. Their top scorer Killian Phillips is already in the golden-boot conversation with a healthy tally this early in the season. Excellent first-half form (best home PPG at half-table) before a slight dip.

Standout Players
